Privacy Policy – Musmach

Draft. Have a lawyer review it before publishing. Last updated: October 8, 2026

The "Musmach" app is operated by Mohammad Garra ("we"). This policy explains what we collect and why.

What we collect

  • Email address and password (the password is stored hashed by our authentication provider).
  • Study activity: your answers, saved questions, mock exam results, error reports and the exam date you choose.
  • A device identifier and model, to limit an account to two devices.
  • Website visit counting: which page was opened, the site you came from, device type, language and time zone, with a random id kept in your browser. No cookies, no IP address, no name or email.
  • Subscription status. Payment is handled by Apple or Google; we never receive your card details.

Why

To run your account and subscription, save your progress, show statistics, improve the questions and prevent account sharing.

Service providers

  • Supabase – data storage and sign-in.
  • RevenueCat – subscription management.
  • Apple and Google – payments.

We do not sell personal data or use it for advertising.

Retention and deletion

Data is kept while your account exists. You can delete your account and all its data at any time: Account → Delete account.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete your data by writing to mhmed6214@gmail.com.

Minors

The app is intended for adult nursing students and graduates.
